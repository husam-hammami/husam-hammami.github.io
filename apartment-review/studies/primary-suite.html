A larger bedroom for a family

Annex the quiet end of the private hall to the main bedroom. A second bedroom and a shared main bathroom keep their own doors for children, relatives or overnight guests.

The main bathroom sits between the two bedrooms. Bringing the private hall into the suite gives the bed, dressing area and balcony sitting room one continuous floor.

Privacy from the hall, daylight by the balcony

A walnut screen shields the pillows from the hall. The existing side windows light the bed and writing desk. A low indigo divan opens onto the shared balcony. Guests can still reach the salon and guest washroom independently.

What changes

The two bedrooms total 28 m² today. With the hall and part of the bathroom, the suite has about 43 m² excluding its en-suite. The basin moves along the same wall. The apartment would have one independent bedroom. On site, check wall supports, drainage and bathroom ventilation.

Room for the household

The main bedroom grows from 14.2 to 18.7 m². A 1.80 m bed sits off the entrance sightline, with fitted walnut wardrobes along the inside wall. The existing side window lights the dressing area.

Guests and family keep their privacy

The second bedroom keeps its 13.8 m² and its own door. Its chair shifts toward the balcony to clear the foot of the bed. Both bathrooms keep their fixtures and access. On site, confirm which parts of the corridor partition can open.
